2. Scope

This handbook is the single source of truth for statements made to customers about warranty periods, channel discounts, certification status and delivery lead times for the HC series battery cabinets, the INV series inverters and the in-house EMS platform. Where a customer quotation contradicts this handbook, this handbook prevails until a written exception is issued by Commercial Operations.

4.3 Exclusions

  1. Damage caused by installation that does not follow the official installation manual, including inadequate clearance or unsealed cable entries.
  2. Operation outside the specified ambient range of −20 °C to +50 °C.
  3. Any repair or firmware modification performed by a party that is not an accredited service partner.
  4. Consumables: air filters, door seals and the coolant charge, all of which are treated as scheduled maintenance items.
  5. Sites where the EMS platform has been disconnected from the internet for more than 90 consecutive days, because degradation data cannot be verified.
Response commitment: for a warranty claim raised through the partner portal, the company responds within 2 business days and attends site within 5 business days in a metropolitan area, or 10 business days in a regional area.

5.3 Cancellation and return

  • An order may be cancelled without charge until manufacturing release, which is 10 business days after order confirmation.
  • After manufacturing release a cancellation fee of 15% of order value applies.
  • Commissioned units cannot be returned; the warranty replacement process applies instead.
